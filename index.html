About

Hi, I'm Haven.

I create digital products and services that are thoughtful, functional, and beautifully designed, including booking and portfolio websites. I have a bachelor’s degree in UX/UI design and a minor in graphic design. My workflow includes tools like Figma, VS Code, Claude Code, and the Adobe suite.

Every project starts with design decisions I make myself: layout, user flow, accessibility, and visual direction.

I help small businesses grow with standout visuals and websites that are easy for everyone to use. Whether you need a portfolio to showcase your work or a booking site that brings in new clients, I’ll build something that fits your brand and works for your customers.

Transparency

My AI policy

How I use AI

I use AI tools to help with parts of my process, like drafting and checking code, organising ideas, and testing that sites are easy for everyone to use. I review, edit and approve everything before it reaches you. The design decisions are always mine.

What I don’t do

  • I don’t pass off AI-generated images as real photography or original artwork without telling you.
  • I don’t use AI to copy another business’s branding, writing or designs.
  • I don’t let AI make decisions about your brand without you.

AI-generated images

When you work with me, you decide whether or not to include AI-generated images. We’ll talk this through during your consultation.

I have extensive experience with Photoshop and the Adobe suite, and we can use those skills instead of AI to get the best image quality. If you’d rather not use AI image generation, I’ll need high-quality images from you.

Your content and privacy

I don’t put your private files, client details or unpublished work into AI tools without your permission.

It’s your choice

If you’d rather I didn’t use AI on your project, just tell me and I’ll work without it. Questions are always welcome.
