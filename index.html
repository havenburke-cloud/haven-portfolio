About

Hi, I'm Haven.

I design websites for small businesses and independent creatives who want an online home that feels as polished as the work they do.

I started designing because I loved helping friends turn their talent into something people could find, book, and remember. Today I work one-on-one with each client, so you always know who is building your site and what comes next.

My style is clean and calm, with plenty of space for your work to shine. I care about the small details, like how a page feels on your phone, how quickly it loads, and how easy it is for a new client to say yes.
